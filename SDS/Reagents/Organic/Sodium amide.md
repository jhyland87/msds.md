### Description

- Sodium amide is an odorless colorless solid. Denser than [[Water|water]].[^1]
- Solid; Commercial form is white to olive-green; Quickly absorbs water and carbon dioxide from atmosphere; \[Merck Index\] White solid with an odor of ammonia; \[Hawley\] Grey powder or granules with an ammoniacal odor; \[Alfa Aesar MSDS\][^2]

### Recommended use and restrictions

- **Sources/Uses:** Used as a dehydrating agent, in ammonolysis reactions, in Claisen condensations, and for alkylation of nitriles and ketones; Also used to make indigo, hydrazine, ethynyl compounds, acetylenic carbinols, and sodium cyanide; \[Merck Index\][^2]
- DEHYDRATING AGENT; PRODN OF INDIGO & HYDRAZINE; INTERMEDIATE IN PREPN OF SODIUM CYANIDE; IN AMMONOLYSIS REACTIONS; IN CLAISEN CONDENSATIONS, ALKYLATION OF NITRILES & KETONES, SYNTH OF ETHYNYL CMPD, ACETYLENIC CARBINOLS[^3]

## 5. Fire-Fighting Measures

### Explosion hazards

- WHEN EXPOSED TO ATMOSPHERE, SODIUM AMIDE RAPIDLY ABSORBS [[Water|WATER]] & CARBON DIOXIDE. WHEN ONLY LIMITED ABSORPTION TAKES PLACE, AS IN POORLY SEALED CONTAINERS, PRODUCTS ARE FORMED WHICH RENDER RESULTING MIXT HIGHLY EXPLOSIVE. THE FORMATION OF OXIDATION PRODUCTS IS ACCOMPANIED BY DEVELOPMENT OF YELLOW OR BROWNISH COLOR. IF SUCH CHANGE IS NOTICED, SUBSTANCE SHOULD BE DESTROYED AT ONCE ... BY COVERING WITH MUCH BENZENE, TOLUENE, OR KEROSENE & SLOWLY ADDING DILUTE ETHANOL WITH STIRRING.[^3]

## 10. Stability and Reactivity

### Air and water reactions

- Highly flammable. Reacts violently with [[Water|water]] and bursts into flame.[^1]

### Reactive groups

- Bases, Strong Nitrides, Phosphides, Carbides, and Silicides[^1]

### Reactivity profile

- SODIUM AMIDE is a powerful reducing agent. Reacts violently with oxidizing agents. Reacts violently with steam and water to form caustic NaOH and NH3 vapors \[Bergstrom et al., Chem. Reviews, 12:6 1932\]. May form explosive compounds in the presence of water and carbon dioxide \[Handling Chemicals Safely 1980 p 826\]. Liable to deflagration upon heating and friction. Forms an explosive peroxide on storage. When sodium amide and chromic anhydride are mixed together a vigorous reaction results; the same occurs with other oxidizing agents including dinitrogen tetraoxide, potassium chlorate, sodium nitrite. \[Mellor 11:234 1946-47\]. …[^1]

### Hazardous reactions and incompatibilities

- REACTS VIOLENTLY WITH WATER FORMING SODIUM HYDROXIDE AND AMMONIA[^3]
