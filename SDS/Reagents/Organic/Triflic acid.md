### Hazards summary

- Highly corrosive to skin; \[Quick CPC\] One of the strongest acids known. May cause burns; \[Merck Index\] Oral LD50 (rat) is between 500 and 2,000 mg/kg; An acute dermal toxicity study of rats at 2,000 mg/kg produced extensive and severe local skin effects considered to be life threatening; 50 mg/kg doses were tolerated and showed no significant adverse effects at necropsy; No evidence of skin sensitization in guinea pigs; No evidence of mutagenicity; \[EPA TSCA\] Causes burns; Inhalation of high concentration can cause chemical pneumonitis; \[Alfa Aesar MSDS\][^1]
