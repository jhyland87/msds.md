### Description

- 1,1'-azodi-(hexahydrobenzonitrile) appears as a white to light colored solid substance. Insoluble in [[Water|water]] and more dense that water. May cause irritation to skin, eyes, and mucous membranes. May be toxic by ingestion. If exposed to high temperatures or flames this material may ignite and burn with an intense flame. Dust may form an explosive mixture in air.[^1]

### Reactivity profile
