### Hazards summary

- Apart from their instability in air and their caustic basic properties, organomagnesium compounds and the precipitated inorganic magnesium salts produced during their use display no pronounced toxicity or side effects of environmental concern. \[Ullmann\] Dry powders ignite in air; Solids and ethereal solutions ignite in [[Water|water]]; \[HSDB\] See [[Methylmagnesium chloride]].[^1]
