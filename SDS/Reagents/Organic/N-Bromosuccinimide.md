## 10. Stability and Reactivity

### Chemical stability

- Stable under recommended storage conditions.[^2]

### Hazardous decomposition

- Melting range 172-178 °C (decomposes).[^2]
- When heated to decomposition it emits toxic fumes of Br- and NOx.[^2]

### Hazardous reactions and incompatibilities

- Violent reaction with dibenzoyl peroxide + 4-toluic acid.[^2]
- Explosive reaction with propiononitrile after heating to 105 °C for 24 hr.[^2]
- Reacts explosively with aniline, diallyl sulfide, and hydrazine hydrate.[^2]
- Incompatible materials: Strong oxidizing agents, Strong acids, Strong bases, Iron and iron salts.[^2]

### Other hazardous reactions

- Conditions to avoid: Exposure to light. Exposure to moisture.[^2]

## 11. Toxicological Information

### Toxicity summary

- IDENTIFICATION AND USE: N-Bromosuccinimide is a versatile brominating agent. It is a known protein reagent able to modify amino acids and proteins, resulting in oxidation of tryptophan, tyrosine and histidine residues, as well as sulfhydryl, alcohol and phenol groups. These properties make it a suitable reagent to selectively block certain amino acid residues in biochemistry, and also permit the histochemical detection of proteins by oxidative deamination followed by the Schiff reaction.
