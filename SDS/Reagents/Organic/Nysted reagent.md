### Hazards summary

- Flammable; A mild irritant; May decompose to form flammable or explosive mixtures with air; Lethal-dose study of rats at 2,000 mg/kg produced diarrhea on the day of administration and no deaths; Lethal-concentration study of rats at 20,030 mg/m3 (4,886 ppm) caused no clinical signs of toxicity or irritation and no deaths; \[Air Products MSDS\] May contain up to 3% tetrahydrofuran (with BHT inhibitor) as residual impurity of production; Highly flammable; An irritant; Harmful by ingestion; Targets kidneys, liver, and CNS; \[Aldrich MSDS\][^1]
