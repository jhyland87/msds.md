### Description

- Perchloryl fluoride is a colorless, non corrosive gas with a characteristic sweet odor. Contact with the material may cause irritation to skin, eyes, and mucous membranes. It is very toxic by inhalation and skin absorption. Under prolonged exposure to fire or intense heat the containers may violently rupture and rocket.[^1]
- COLOURLESS GAS OR COMPRESSED LIQUEFIED GAS WITH CHARACTERISTIC ODOUR.[^2]

### Recommended use and restrictions

- **Sources/Uses:** Used as a fluorinating agent in chemical syntheses; \[ACGIH\][^3]
- IN ORGANIC SYNTHESIS TO INTRODUCE [[Fluorine|FLUORINE]] ATOMS INTO ORGANIC MOLECULE; AS OXIDIZING AGENT; AS INSULATOR FOR HIGH VOLTAGE SYSTEMS[^4]

## 10. Stability and Reactivity

### Hazardous decomposition

- WHEN HEATED TO DECOMP, IT EMITS HIGHLY TOXIC FUMES OF CHLORIDES & FLUORIDES.[^4]

### Corrosivity

- DOES NOT CORRODE BASE METALS WHEN ANHYDROUS[^4]

### Chemical dangers

- The substance is a strong oxidant. It reacts violently with combustible and reducing materials. This generates fire and explosion hazard. Reacts violently with nitrogenous bases and finely divided organic materials. This generates fire and explosion hazard. Attacks some forms of plastic, rubber and coatings.[^2]

### Air and water reactions

- Slightly soluble in [[Water|water]].[^1]

### Reactive groups

- Oxidizing Agents, Strong Halogenating Agents[^1]

### Reactivity profile

- PERCHLORYL FLUORIDE is a propellant; a powerful oxidant. It ignites upon contact with alcohols, amines, ammonia, beryllium alkyls, boranes, dicyanogen, hydrazines, hydrocarbons, hydrogen, nitroalkanes, powdered metals, silanes, or thiols \[Bretherick 1979. p.174\]. It may generate hydrofluoric acid if it comes into contact with water.[^1]

### Hazardous reactions and incompatibilities

- Combustibles, strong bases, amines, finely divided metals, reducing agents, alcohols[^12]
- POWERFUL OXIDIZING AGENT.[^4]
- Preparation of dimethylaminomethyl-2-fluoroferrocene, by interaction of the lithio compound in THF at -70 °C with perchloryl fluoride diluted with helium, unexpectedly exploded violently.[^4]

## 11. Toxicological Information

### Inhalation risk

- A harmful concentration of this gas in the air will be reached very quickly on loss of containment.[^2]

### Short-term exposure

- The substance is irritating to the respiratory tract. Inhalation of this gas may cause lung oedema. Inhalation of high concentrations may cause formation of methaemoglobin. The liquid may cause frostbite. Medical observation is indicated. The effects may be delayed.[^2]

### Long-term exposure

- The substance may have effects on the blood. This may result in the formation of methaemoglobin. May cause fluorosis.[^2]

### Routes of exposure

- The substance can be absorbed into the body by inhalation.[^2]
- inhalation, skin and/or eye contact (liquid)[^12]

### Signs and symptoms

- **Inhalation Exposure:** Cough. Sore throat. Laboured breathing. Shortness of breath.[^2]
- **Skin Exposure:** ON CONTACT WITH LIQUID: FROSTBITE.[^2]
- irritation respiratory system; liquid: frostbite; In Animals: methemoglobinemia; cyanosis; lassitude (weakness, exhaustion), dizziness, headache; pulmonary edema; pneumonitis; anoxia[^12]

### Target organs

- Skin, respiratory system, blood[^12]
