### Description

- Methyl bromoacetate appears as a colorless to straw-colored liquid with a sharp penetrating odor. Denser than [[Water|water]] and soluble in water. Severly irritates skin and eyes. Toxic by ingestion and inhalation. Used to make vitamins and pharmaceuticals.[^1]
- Colorless to straw-colored liquid with a penetrating odor; \[CAMEO\] Clear light yellow liquid; \[Sigma-Aldrich MSDS\][^2]

### Recommended use and restrictions

- **Sources/Uses:** Used to make pharmaceuticals and vitamins; \[CAMEO\][^2]
- Synthesis of weed killers, dyes, vitamins, pharmaceuticals; lachrymator[^3]
- Methyl bromoacetate was used in the synthesis of novel coumarins. It was also employed in the synthesis of cis-cyclopropanes.[^3]

### Reactive groups

- Esters, Sulfate Esters, Phosphate Esters, Thiophosphate Esters, and Borate Esters Halogenated Organic Compounds[^1]

### Reactivity profile

- METHYL BROMOACETATE is a halogenated ester. Esters react with acids to liberate heat along with alcohols and acids. Strong oxidizing acids may cause a vigorous reaction that is sufficiently exothermic to ignite the reaction products. Heat is also generated by the interaction of esters with caustic solutions. Flammable hydrogen is generated by mixing esters with alkali metals and hydrides.[^1]

### Other hazardous reactions

- Conditions to avoid: Heat, flames and sparks. Incompatible materials: acids, bases, oxidizing agents, reducing agents.[^3]
