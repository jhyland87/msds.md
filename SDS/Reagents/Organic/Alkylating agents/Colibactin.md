## 11. Toxicological Information

### Toxicity summary

- This publication describes bacterial genotoxins that can cause DNA damage by introducing single- and double-strand DNA breaks in the host cells, e.g., cytolethal distending toxin (CDT) and the peptide-polyketide genotoxin colibactin produced by the phylogenetic group B2 of E. coli. These E. coli-produced genotoxins impair host DNA, which results in senescence or apoptosis of cells if the damage is beyond repair, or the cells can survive and proliferate if the genotoxin-induced DNA damage is not severe enough to kill them, and then the surviving cells may accumulate genomic instability and acquire malignant traits.[^4]
