## 10. Stability and Reactivity

### Air and water reactions

- Highly flammable. Insoluble in [[Water|water]].[^1]

### Reactive groups

- Sulfonates, Phosphonates, and Thiophosphonates, Organic Fluorinated Organic Compounds[^1]

### Reactivity profile

- METHYL TRIFLUOROMETHANE SULFONATE is sensitive to heat. (NTP, 1992). Esters react with acids to liberate heat along with alcohols and acids. Strong oxidizing acids may cause a vigorous reaction that is sufficiently exothermic to ignite the reaction products. Heat is also generated by the interaction of esters with caustic solutions. Flammable hydrogen is generated by mixing esters with alkali metals and hydrides.[^1]
