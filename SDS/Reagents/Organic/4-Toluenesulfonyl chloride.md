- P-toluene sulfonylchloride appears as a white to gray powdered solid with a distinctive odor. Insoluble in [[Water|water]] and denser than water. Contact may irritate skin, eyes and mucous membranes. May be toxic by ingestion, inhalation and skin absorption. Used to make other chemicals.[^1]
