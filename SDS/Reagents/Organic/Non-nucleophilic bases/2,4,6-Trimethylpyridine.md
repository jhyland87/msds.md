### Description

- Collidine appears as a clear colorless liquid. Less dense than [[Water|water]] and insoluble in water. Hence floats on water. Vapors heavier than air. Produces toxic oxides of nitrogen during combustion. Used to make other chemicals.[^1]
- Colorless liquid with an aromatic odor; \[HSDB\] Colorless liquid with an unpleasant odor; \[Alfa Aesar MSDS\][^2]

### Recommended use and restrictions

- **Sources/Uses:** Used as a chemical intermediate, dehydrohalogenation agent, tissue fixative for electron microscopy, and solvent for the manufacture of anthraquinones; \[HSDB\][^2]
