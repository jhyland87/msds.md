### Description

- Trimethylacetyl chloride appears as colorless fuming liquid with a pungent odor. Very toxic by inhalation, ingestion or skin absorption. Fumes irritate the eyes and mucous membranes. Corrosive to most metals and tissue.[^1]

## 10. Stability and Reactivity

### Air and water reactions

- Highly flammable. Fumes in air. The liquid reacts vigorously and exothermically with [[Water|water]] to form trimethylacetic acid and corrosive [[Hydrochloric acid (32-38%)|hydrochloric acid]]; both acids corrode metals and tissue.[^1]

### Reactive groups

- Acyl Halides, Sulfonyl Halides, and Chloroformates[^1]

### Reactivity profile

- TRIMETHYLACETYL CHLORIDE is acidic. Incompatible with bases (including amines), strong oxidizing agents, and alcohols. May react vigorously or explosively if mixed with diisopropyl ether or other ethers in the presence of trace amounts of metal salts \[J. Haz. Mat., 1981, 4, 291\].[^1]
