### Description

- Thioacetic acid appears as a clear,yellow liquid with a strong, unpleasant odor. May be toxic by ingestion, inhalation and skin absorption. Irritating to skin and eyes. Used to make other chemicals.[^1]
- Yellow liquid with a pungent odor; \[Merck Index\] Colorless or pale yellow liquid; \[Alfa Aesar MSDS\][^2]

### Recommended use and restrictions

- **Sources/Uses:** Used to make other chemicals; \[CAMEO\] Used as a source of thiol groups in molecular synthesis and as a component of Schiff's reagent; \[REPROTOX\] Used as a flavoring agent; \[FDA\][^2]

## 10. Stability and Reactivity

### Air and water reactions

- Highly flammable. [[Water]] soluble.[^1]

### Reactive groups

- Acids, Carboxylic Peroxides, Organic[^1]

### Reactivity profile

- THIOACETIC ACID is a thio organic acid. Organosulfides are incompatible with acids, diazo and azo compounds, halocarbons, isocyanates, aldehydes, alkali metals, nitrides, hydrides, and other strong reducing agents. Reactions with these materials generate heat and in many cases hydrogen gas. Many of these compounds may liberate hydrogen sulfide upon decomposition or reaction with an acid.[^1]
